aithings / igmd

Export Instagram chats to markdown.

Drop a Meta Download your information ZIP. Get one .md per chat to read, grep, or paste into another model.

Files never leave the browser. There is no server and no account.

    Unknown ZIPs fail with an error — they do not produce empty files. Caps: 50 MB ZIP, 200 entries, 50 MB JSON. Messages are written oldest-first. Media is skipped.
