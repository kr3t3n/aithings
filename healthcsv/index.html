aithings / healthcsv

Apple Health, filtered.

Drop export.xml or the Health zip. One click for a preset. Get a compact CSV — not the whole dump.

Files never leave the browser. There is no server and no account.

One click sets types and the date range. Ranges are relative to the latest date in the file, not the computer clock. “This year” is 1 January of that year through that latest date. You can still tick extra types or change the range after a preset.

    Caps: warn if the zip is over 80 MB or export.xml inside is over 200 MB (a 107 MB zip with a ~1 GB export.xml is streamed, not refused). Refuse only a zip over 500 MB compressed, or a zip with no export.xml. export_cda.xml and workout-routes are skipped. Record and Workout only.
